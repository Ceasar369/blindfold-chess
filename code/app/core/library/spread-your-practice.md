# Spread your practice

Spread the same practice over many days, rather than a few long blocks.

Return to what I missed on a later day, not straight away.

Proven: Rohrer 2015, a review: spreading the same teaching over longer periods raised scores a month or more later; Dunlosky 2013, a review: distributed practice has high utility; Cepeda 2006, 317 experiments: the gap between sessions shapes retention. All on school or lab material, not chess. Applying it to chess is my preferred approach.
