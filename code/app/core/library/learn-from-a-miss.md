# Learn from a miss

After a miss, read the right answer and why it is right.

Being told only that I was wrong teaches little.

Find the step where my reasoning went wrong, not only the answer.

Proven: Van der Kleij 2015, a meta-analysis of 40 studies of computer feedback: an explanation averaged 0.49, the right answer 0.32, right-or-wrong only 0.05; Metcalfe 2017, a review: analysing the reasoning behind a mistake helps. School and college material, not chess. Applying it to chess is my preferred approach.
