# Knight destinations

A knight jumps to the opposite corner of a 2×3 rectangle.

Picture that rectangle instead of counting two, then one.

Example: a knight on f4 reaches d3, d5, e2, e6, g2, g6, h3 and h5.

Gradually see all its squares at once, without the rectangle.

This is my preferred approach, from DarkSquares' Knight moves and Chess.com's Knight Sight. Unverified: no study tests it.
